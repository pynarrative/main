What to do now?

  1. Download your template!

  2. Cut the .py file and paste it into the following directory: pynarrative > src > pynarrative > templates > mytemplates

  3. (If you want) download your example notebook and enjoy!

  4. On your python script or on your notebook write:

    from pynarrative.templates.mytemplates. import

  5. Try it and enjoy!

    Important!
